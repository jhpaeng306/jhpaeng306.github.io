2023

  1. Coordinate-Update Algorithms can Efficiently Detect Infeasible Optimization Problems
    Jinhee Paeng, Jisun Park, and Ernest K. Ryu
    Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications, 2023
    Preprint, under minor revision
    Abs arXiv Bib Video Slides

    Analysis of the asymptotic behavior of randomized coordinate fixed-point iterations (RC-FPI) without any fixed points. We prove almost sure convergence of the normalized iterates to the negative infimal displacement vector and establish an asymptotic upper bound on the variance, which yields an infeasibility detection method for randomized FPI-type algorithms.

2021

  1. Applications of Renormalization Group Method for Generalized Kuramoto Type Models
    Jinhee Paeng, Kibeom Myoung, Seungmok Lee, Seung Yeal Ha, and Jaeyoung Yoon
    2021
    Preprint
    Abs Bib PDF Poster

    We apply the renormalization group method to the Lohe sphere model to prove that the completely synchronized state among particles with identical natural frequencies forms a stable state.
